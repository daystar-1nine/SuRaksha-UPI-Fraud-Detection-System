# SECTION D: 20 TOUGHEST JUDGE QUESTIONS & DEFENSES

#### 1. How does Suraksha detect if a QR code is forged or swapped?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"We use a two-step validation: First, we evaluate the URI format and match the payee handle against known scam lists. Second, for registered merchants, we verify an HMAC SHA-256 digital signature embedded in the QR parameters. If a scammer overlays a fake QR sticker, the signature fails and Suraksha instantly flags the transaction."*
* **Code Evidence:** `services/qr_risk_analyzer.py` & `routes/qr.py`.
* **What NOT to say:** *"We have a magic AI that knows all QR codes."*

#### 2. What is Error Level Analysis (ELA) and why is it reliable for receipts?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"When an image is saved as a JPEG, each 8x8 block reaches a uniform compression state. If someone edits the text in Photoshop to fake a transaction amount, those modified pixels have different error levels upon resaving. Suraksha resaves the image in memory at 75% quality, calculates the difference matrix, and amplifies it by 18x to reveal the forged region."*
* **Code Evidence:** `services/tamper_detector.py` (`io.BytesIO` pipeline).
* **What NOT to say:** *"It’s 100% infallible on every compressed image."*

#### 3. What about false positives on heavily compressed WhatsApp images?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"That’s why we do not rely on ELA alone. Suraksha uses multi-signal fusion: ELA error ratio is combined with Laplacian edge variance, metadata timestamps, and OCR text matching. If an image is just heavily compressed, it receives a 'Caution' score rather than a hard block, prompting the merchant to check their actual bank SMS."*
* **Code Evidence:** `services/master_engine.py` (Weighted metric scoring).
* **What NOT to say:** *"Our system never makes mistakes."*

#### 4. How do you respect user privacy if they upload personal payment screenshots?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Suraksha enforces a Zero-Data Retention policy. Image byte streams are ingested exclusively into volatile RAM using Python's `io.BytesIO` buffer, stripped of EXIF GPS tags, processed for ELA metrics, and immediately cleared from memory. No user gallery photos are saved to disk."*
* **Code Evidence:** `services/tamper_detector.py`.
* **What NOT to say:** *"We store screenshots to train our big database."*

#### 5. Why wouldn't Google Pay or PhonePe build this themselves?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Payment apps optimize for minimal checkout friction and maximum transaction throughput; adding heavy cross-platform receipt forensics or external message scanning falls outside their primary core loop. Suraksha operates as an independent security layer that can either serve users directly or be licensed to fintechs as an integrated Risk-as-a-Service SDK."*
* **What NOT to say:** *"Google Pay and PhonePe don't care about security."*

#### 6. What is your competitive moat?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Our moat is three-fold: 1) Multi-modal synthesis (combining QR crypto, ELA image forensics, and NLP), 2) Sub-200ms edge-compatible processing, and 3) A crowdsourced, localized fraud intelligence database tailored specifically to Indian regional payment patterns."*
* **What NOT to say:** *"We have patents that nobody can touch."*

#### 7. How does the NLP classifier work on SMS/WhatsApp messages?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"We use a Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier trained on payment phishing patterns, combined with regex heuristic matrices that check for high-urgency keywords, fake KYC alerts, and debit-intent deep links (`upi://pay`). It executes in under 20 milliseconds."*
* **Code Evidence:** `services/ml_classifier.py`.

#### 8. How will you acquire your first 1,000 users?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"We are starting with a hyper-local B2B pilot targeting retail merchant associations and local shopkeepers in Tier-2/3 market corridors who frequently face fake receipt scams. We provide verified counter QR stands that build customer trust while acquiring initial users."*

#### 9. What is your primary revenue model?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"A tiered model: Free B2C consumer scanner, ₹99/month B2B merchant verification subscription (including verified QR stands and screenshot verification), and usage-based B2B2C API licensing for fintech payment aggregators."*

#### 10. Can scammers bypass your HMAC QR signature?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"No, because HMAC SHA-256 requires the merchant's secret key stored securely on our server. An attacker pasting a sticker cannot forge a valid signature without the merchant's private key."*

#### 11. What happens if a merchant isn't registered on your platform?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Unregistered merchants revert to heuristic evaluation: format validation, VPA blacklist checks, and behavioral risk scoring, providing a baseline risk rating (e.g., 30% Caution) without blocking normal peer-to-peer payments."*

#### 12. How does the dynamic Hindi translation work without breaking the UI?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"In `js/language.js`, we pre-sort translation keys by string length descending to match complete phrases first, and enforce regex word boundaries (`\b`) on single words to eliminate accidental sub-word replacements."*
* **Code Evidence:** `js/language.js`.

#### 13. How do you prevent database locking when thousands of users submit reports?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"We implemented a custom connection manager with a 20-second timeout hook (`sqlite3.connect(timeout=20.0)`) and auto-commit/rollback wrappers, preventing `OperationalError` lockups under concurrent load."*
* **Code Evidence:** `services/history_store.py`.

#### 14. What dataset did you evaluate your system on?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"We tested the system across 4,300 benchmark samples: 1,500 QR payloads, 800 transaction receipt images (400 authentic, 400 tampered), and 2,000 text notification strings."*

#### 15. What are your current technical limitations?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Screenshots that have been re-compressed multiple times via messaging apps can cause elevated ELA noise. We mitigate this by fusing OCR text extraction with Laplacian edge variance rather than relying on ELA alone."*

#### 16. Is this an Android app or a Web app?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"It is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) using Vite and Tailwind, and packaged as a native Android APK using Capacitor to trigger native Android payment intents (`GPay`, `PhonePe`)."*
* **Code Evidence:** `capacitor.config.json` & `build_android.ps1`.

#### 17. How fast does the analysis run?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Average latency is 194 milliseconds: QR inspection takes 4ms, NLP text parsing takes 12ms, and in-memory ELA forensics takes 178ms—all completing well before a user types their PIN."*

#### 18. What is your team’s background and ability to execute?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"Our core team brings hands-on engineering expertise in computer vision (OpenCV), backend microservices (Python/Flask), and front-end PWA development. We built and tested this working prototype from scratch."*

#### 19. What is your plan if NPCI introduces built-in QR signing?
* **Best Answer (30s):** *"If NPCI introduces mandatory QR signing, that accelerates our market! Suraksha’s verification engine will immediately validate NPCI certs while continuing to provide receipt forensics, phishing text analysis, and merchant POS protection."*
